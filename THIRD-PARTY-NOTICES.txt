ASP.NET Core - Microsoft.AspNetCore.Components.AI (0.1.0-preview at commit 31b20463068f8d9ad900393bf96c9a182c397216)
========================================================================

https://github.com/dotnet/aspnetcore/tree/31b20463068f8d9ad900393bf96c9a182c397216/src/Components/AI

Portions of Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor were adapted from
Microsoft.AspNetCore.Components.AI. Specifically:

- The CSS design tokens, button styles, message list layout, bubble sizing, typing
  dots animation, error banner, and chat-page shell in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/wwwroot/mchat.css are
  adapted from src/Components/AI/src/wwwroot/ai-chat.css. Class names were renamed
  from sc-ai-* to mchat-* and additional tokens were added for provider-neutral
  participant chrome.

- The MessageContentRenderer<TContent> registration pattern in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/Components/MessageContentRenderer.cs
  is adapted from src/Components/AI/src/Components/BlockRenderer.cs.

- The ChatViewContext cascade and region-keyed lifetime pattern in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/Components/ChatViewContext.cs
  are adapted from src/Components/AI/src/Components/MessageListContext.cs and
  src/Components/AI/src/Components/AgentBoundary.cs.

- The Header/body/footer three-region shell in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/Components/ChatView.razor is
  adapted from src/Components/AI/src/Components/ChatPage.cs.

- The scroll-container + streaming/error footer layout in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/Components/ChatMessagesView.razor
  is adapted from src/Components/AI/src/Components/MessageList.cs.

- The textarea Enter-to-submit / Shift+Enter-for-newline pattern in
  src/AIExtensions/Microsoft.Maui.Chat.Controls.Blazor/Components/Composer.razor is
  adapted from src/Components/AI/src/Components/MessageInput.cs.

The upstream AI engine (blocks, turns, UIAgent, AgentContext) is NOT copied. This
package is provider-neutral by design.
