# Playground field notes

The blue bird rests near a meadow stream where willows shade the water. Its song
is most noticeable at sunrise, when the air is cool and the path is quiet.

On a nearby hill, a small observatory tracks the night sky. Visitors can learn
about planets, stars, and the changing phases of the moon.

A garden workshop teaches how to grow basil and tomatoes in containers. Plants
need sunlight, water, and healthy soil throughout the growing season.
